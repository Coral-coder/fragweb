Arena deathmatch in your browser. No install. Grab your friends.

Controls & how it works
  • WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · Click fire
  • 1–5 or wheel switch weapons · Tab scores · T chat · Esc menu
  • Rocket-jump: look down, jump and fire at your feet.
  • The host's browser runs the match. Keep the host's game open for the whole session.
  • Everyone needs internet briefly to find each other; gameplay then goes directly between the PCs.
